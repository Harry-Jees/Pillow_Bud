# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Pillow Bud is intended to support emotional well-being. We aim to make participation welcoming and respectful for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Accept good-faith feedback gracefully.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Protect users' privacy; never post personal conversations, journal entries, credentials, or other sensitive data.
- Discuss mental-health safety issues with care and without sensationalism.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, unwanted sexual attention, personal attacks, and publishing private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close discussions, or block participants for behavior that violates this code. Report concerns privately to the repository maintainer.
